FAQ

Questions? Answered.

The things trade business owners usually ask us first.

How fast is "7 days," really?
Seven days from the moment we have your business details — services, service area, and any photos or logo you have. (Don't have photos? We'll use quality placeholders and swap them later.)
I'm not tech-savvy. Is that a problem?
Not at all. We handle everything — design, writing, setup, and launch. You just answer a short questionnaire and hop on one 15-minute call.
What if I don't like the first draft?
Then we revise it until you do. You only pay the remaining balance once you've approved the site — that's our guarantee.
Do you write the content for me?
Yes. We draft all the text from your questionnaire answers, in plain language your customers understand. You review and approve everything before launch.
Will my site show up on Google?
Every site ships with SEO fundamentals: proper titles, local service-area pages, fast load times, and mobile-friendly design — the groundwork Google rewards. (Full local-SEO campaigns are a separate conversation.)
Do I need the AI systems to get a website?
No. The website stands on its own, and the AI systems stand on theirs — they work with any Forge website or with a site you already have. Most clients start with the website and add a system once the calls pick up.
